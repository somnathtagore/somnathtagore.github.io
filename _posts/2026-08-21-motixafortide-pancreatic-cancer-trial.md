The CXCR4–CXCL12 axis contributes to immune exclusion in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. This study tested whether disrupting that axis could support an immune response when combined with PD-1 blockade and standard chemotherapy.

The first stage of an open-label, single-arm phase 2 trial evaluated motixafortide, cemiplimab, gemcitabine, and nab-paclitaxel in 11 treatment-naïve patients with metastatic disease. The reported objective response rate was 64%, with 55% confirmed partial responses and a 91% disease-control rate. Median progression-free and overall survival were 9.7 and 10.1 months, respectively.

Single-cell correlatives are especially important here: they connect the clinical intervention to changes in the tumor ecosystem and help clarify which immune and tumor states accompany response. The results are encouraging but should be interpreted in the context of the study's small, non-randomized first stage.

**Journal:** *Nature Communications* (2026)  
**Article:** [doi:10.1038/s41467-026-76559-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76559-4)
