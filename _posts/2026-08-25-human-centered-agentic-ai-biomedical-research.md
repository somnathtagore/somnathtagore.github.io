Agentic AI can do more than generate text: it can plan analyses, use tools, synthesize evidence, and propose next steps. In biomedical research, however, useful autonomy must remain coupled to scientific accountability.

This framework organizes the problem around three linked ideas:

- **scientific agency** — what an AI system may plan or execute, and where human judgment remains essential;
- **evidence** — how claims retain traceable support, uncertainty, and reproducible provenance; and
- **governance** — how permissions, oversight, and responsibility should scale with the consequence of an action.

The goal is not autonomy for its own sake. It is a human-centered research architecture in which agents help scientists move faster while preserving inspectability, methodological rigor, and clear decision ownership.
